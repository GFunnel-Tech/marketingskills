## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Done-for-you marketing and operations for small businesses, with the software included.
**What it does:** GFunnel's team and vetted vendor network run a business's paid ads, funnels and websites, social, outreach, automation and AI employees. Everything is managed inside a GFunnel workspace, which is a CRM, funnels, phone, calendars, Sales Hub, organizer, projects and community in one login. The GFunnel Methodology and its BEAS business score set what gets fixed first.
**Product category:** Done-for-you marketing services / fractional marketing department (buyer's shelf). Secondary shelf: all-in-one business software.

2. **Business owners** who already make money and want more leads and less operational work. Priority vertical **[CONFIRM]**.
3. **Agencies** that serve business owners and need software, white-label fulfillment and margin.

**Partner network:** Any member can earn by marketing their own products, GFunnel's products, or other members' products and services, with multi-tier commissions. Words to avoid: "MLM", "passive income", "guaranteed", and any earnings figure without an income disclosure.

**Primary use case:** Get a predictable flow of leads and booked appointments without hiring a marketing team or juggling five vendors.
**Jobs to be done:**
- "Run my marketing so I can run my business."
- "Give me one place to see leads, calls and what's working."
- "Tell me what to fix first in my business."
**Use cases:** Launching Meta ads with a funnel and automated follow-up. Replacing a patchwork of freelancers. Adding an AI receptionist or sales agent. Migrating off a messy CRM.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Marketing is either DIY, which takes time and skill they lack, or outsourced to several vendors who don't talk to each other.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Agencies are opaque and lock in clients.
- Freelancers need managing.
- Software like GoHighLevel or HubSpot gives tools but no one to run them.
**Cost:** Wasted ad spend, slow lead follow-up, an owner working evenings on marketing.
**Emotional tension:** "I've been burned before," and "I don't know what's actually working."

**Direct (depends on which offer leads; see analysis §5):**

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** An agency they can't see into, too many tools, leads going cold.
**Pull:** One team, software included, AI follow-up, fixed price.
**Habit:** An existing agency contract, sunk cost in their current CRM.
**Anxiety:** Lock-in, migration pain, being "just another GHL reseller." Address this with Migration services and data ownership.
